How it works

{{ icon("database") }}

Learns from history

Trained on U.S. Bureau of Transportation Statistics records ({{ data_years }}): monthly arrivals and delays of every airline at every U.S. airport.

{{ icon("cpu") }}

Compares similar situations

Looks at the airline, the airport, the time of year and how busy the route is, and compares them with similar months in the past.

{{ icon("target") }}

Gives a clear answer

Shows a risk level, how sure it is and what to do. On past records it had never seen, it was right about 6 times out of 10; when wrong, it usually picks the next level.

{{ icon("info") }} This tool supports planning decisions. It does not include live weather or air-traffic information.
